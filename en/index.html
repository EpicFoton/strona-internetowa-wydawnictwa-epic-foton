Epic Foton is a publishing house founded in 2026 with the aim of publishing the graphic novel Cave of Stars as well as upcoming titles in our graphic novel lineup.




In 2019, following the completion of the script, the project was joined by Jacek Frąś - a comic book illustrator acclaimed both in Poland and abroad.

Initiated by Sebastian Wach in 2011, the project brought on board acclaimed comic book writer Grzegorz Janusz.




czy wiedziałeś, że?

"Imagine a dead Albert Einstein. Ready? Now for something harder. Imagine two dead Einsteins! Identical as twins! For one Einstein, we hold a solemn funeral here on Earth, while the second, entangled with the first, we bury on another planet. Thousands of light-years away. If, upon hearing the results of Aspect's experiment, this Earthly Einstein turns over in his grave… then that second Einstein, buried in a distant galaxy, will also instantly flip from his back to his stomach."

16+

The first volume of this metaphysical speculative saga follows Leon Szarak as he attempts to rebuild his life and understand the world after a devastating tragedy. It is a tale about searching for the meaning of suffering in a reality far more complex than common sense dictates. Paradoxically, science especially quantum physics opens doors to unimaginable new dimensions, inspiring both philosophers and physicists. Where lie the boundaries of human spirituality?
